MegaBase Builder: Custom Base

Start a blank base, draw rooms on a grid from 5 × 5 up to 100 × 100, stack floors above and below ground, add stairways, and place decor one item at a time.

Start a new blank base

New bases start with one empty ground floor. You can add floors above and below ground later.

Decor and foliage

Side view

Columns are tiles and rows are panels. Panels are drawn taller than tiles so every floor stays readable. Click a room or floor to select it. Drag a room sideways to move it, or onto another floor to change its floor. Drag the square handles on a selected room to change its length or height. Drag the bar on a floor's edge to change that floor's panel height. Delete removes the selected room.

Floors

The ground floor can't be removed. A floor in the middle of the stack can't be removed on its own, because the floors above it would be left with no way up (you can remove it together with them). Floor height is in panels and sets the default height of every room on that floor. Floors and rooms can override the base material.

Stairways

Draw a stairway on the plan with the Draw stairs tool, then pick which floors it connects in the Selected panel.
